# Community expectations

Be respectful, specific and evidence-led. Technical disagreement is welcome; harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and attacks on contributors are not. Respect pseudonyms and personal boundaries. Do not pressure anyone to join calls, disclose personal information, provide copyrighted files or work without credit.

The coordinator may moderate issues, request changes or restrict participation to protect contributors and the project. Raise sensitive conduct concerns through a private channel established by the maintainer; public complaints should omit private details. Decisions should be proportionate and explain the behavior at issue.
